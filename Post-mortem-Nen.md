# Post-mortem Nen

A strong Nen user who is killed in a fight is not always finished. Nothing in the game warns you of this, so this page does: if you kill an awakened player of Nen level 25 or higher, their body may get up and come for you, and the fight leaves a mark on you. The stronger they were, the longer the grudge follows you.

## When a death counts

A death counts when all of this is true:

- The victim is awakened, at Nen level 25 or higher, and not in Zetsu, not Nen Severed, not inside a Nen-sealed zone and not in an exam, a training drill or the Hatsu trial.
- The killer is another living thing that is not the victim. A player or a creature that carries aura (a Rogue Nen User) always counts. Any other creature counts only if the victim was in Wrath or was sworn to the Last Will Oath.
- The victim did not die of a blow the killer never chose: Thorns, or the hit-back of an infused piece of armour, makes nobody a killer.
- The victim was not killed by a risen body, an orphaned beast or a risen mob. Their kills never start more of this.
- The victim has not had a counted death in the last 20 minutes, and the same killer has not killed the same victim in the last hour.

What follows lands on the killer, never on a bystander, and it never goes through protection the victim could not break: a body does not rise against a player on a server with PvP off, against a teammate when friendly fire is off, or across a claim that does not allow PvP.

## The Last Movement

Where the victim fell, their body lies still for 2 seconds. There is no message and no sound. Then it gets up in a jerky motion, wearing their skin and their aura, and hunts the one who killed them.

- **One target.** It only ever goes for the killer. It walks straight at them, so a wall can stop it.
- **It is fast.** It moves faster than a sprinting player, so you cannot simply outrun it without Speed, a mount or an ability.
- **It hits hard, and harder over time.** Its blow is 8 damage times the victim's Nen potency at the fall (at most doubled), and it grows by half again by the end. Ten and Ken defend against it as against any hit.
- **Anyone can hit it.** It has 30 health, and plain and Nen damage both hurt it. Your allies can help.
- **It lasts 15 seconds from the fall,** the lying time included. It falls apart sooner if you die or log out, if you leave the dimension, if you get more than 48 blocks away or if it enters a Nen-sealed zone, and when it falls apart it bursts and is gone. It is never saved: a restart removes it.
- **It leaves nothing.** It drops nothing, gives no experience, and a kill it makes gives its owner no credit.
- **It is a real body.** Everyone sees it, and En feels it like any creature. Aura Read shows the dead player's type and their Nen level.

You can end it early with a counter. Aura Severance cuts it down, and so does Exorcism aimed at it (a Specialist's ability); it needs no consent. Killing it also works.

The body's owner gets one line once they respawn: "Your body moved." They earn the advancement **Unfinished Business**. Whoever lives through the body earns **It Got Up**.

## The Death Grudge

A player who kills a counted victim gets the effect **Death Grudge** for 10 minutes. It takes a quarter of your aura regeneration. A different victim's counted death adds one more quarter, up to three victims in all. Killing the same victim again does not add to it.

It survives milk, totems and `/effect clear`. It ends when its time runs out, when you die, when your Nen is reset, when an Exorcist lifts it (it needs your consent, like lifting a curse) or when you use the stone's **Cleanse** on yourself (the same cost as lifting a curse). Players with Gyo can see a dark shimmer on someone who carries it. A mob that kills a counted victim gets no mark.

## The Orphan Beast

If the victim had a Nen beast out when they died, it does not dissolve. For 15 seconds it hunts the killer alone, at the strength it had, and grows like the body does. It is not saved, it follows the same protection rules as the body, and anyone can hit it. See [Nen Beasts](Nen-Beasts).

## Rising Rogues

So that you meet all this before a player does it to you: a Rogue Nen User, or a wild Nen beast, killed by an awakened player has a one in ten chance of getting up again. It rises as a fresh creature of the same kind with half its health, hits harder as the seconds pass, hunts only the player who killed it and falls apart after 15 seconds. It drops nothing and gives no experience, and it cannot rise twice.

## The grudge that follows

A victim earns more effects the higher their Nen level at death. At level 25 a counted death gets everything above plus Allies Feel It, Lingering Loans and hardening; level 30 adds the Cursed Weapon, 35 the Sealed Hatsu and 40 the Haunted Ground (the defaults; a server can change the levels). Each effect has its own on and off key.

### Allies Feel It

The victim's allies (teammates, and members or allies of the same nation) within 64 blocks of the death sense the killer for 60 seconds: an awakened ally sees the killer outlined in dark red, through walls, wherever they go. Only awakened allies can sense, so an ally who has not awakened feels nothing. Zetsu and In hide the killer from it like from any sensing.

### The Cursed Weapon

If the killer struck the fatal blow in melee, the weapon in their hand may take a curse, with a one in two chance. It is an ordinary curse of a kind that fits the weapon, and it stays hidden until it reveals itself like any other, with one line to the holder then. A conjured item, an infused piece and an item that is already cursed never take one. It comes off the usual ways: the Meditation Stone's Cleanse or an Exorcism. See [Cursed Items](Cursed-Items).

### The Sealed Hatsu

A player who kills a victim of level 35 or higher has one ability sealed for 3 minutes. It is the ability that struck the fatal blow when that was a Nen hit, otherwise the killer's best-mastered bound ability. A technique is never sealed. While a seal runs, another kill cannot start a second one or lengthen it. It ends early if you use the Meditation Stone's **Settle** or an Exorcist lifts it (it needs your consent, like lifting a curse). It also ends if your Nen is reset. Creative and spectator players are not sealed.

### The Haunted Ground

A victim of level 40 or higher, killed by a player, leaves a haunted spot where they fell. It is a circle of 6 blocks that lasts 20 minutes (one game day). It acts only on the killer: while they stand in it they lose 2 aura a second, and hostile mobs within 24 blocks turn on them. It spawns nothing and touches nobody else, so a base with a spot in it stays safe, and on claimed land the mobs leave the killer alone. A world keeps at most 32 spots, and a new one within 8 blocks of an old one replaces it. Players with Gyo see it as drifting dust; everyone else sees nothing. An Exorcist who uses Exorcism with no player in sight, close to a spot, clears it. A spot survives a restart.

### Hardened seals and hardened gear

Everything the victim sealed stays harder to break. Every aura seal the victim placed, in every dimension, becomes **hardened**: a breach of it takes twice as long (the seal also keeps the effect if the owner lifts it and seals it again). It happens once; a second death does not harden it further.

Pieces of [Infused Gear](Infused-Gear) the victim made and had in their hands or on their body gain one step of hardening, to a maximum of 2 (a server that wipes Nen at death gives both steps at once). A hardened piece counts as one grade higher, past the level limit. At the second step the piece is also **grudge-bound**: a stranger who wields it pays double the upkeep, and its upkeep is paid even when you are not letting it work. Its tooltip says so in dark red, and a dropped one gives a darker shimmer to Gyo. Exorcism and the stone's Cleanse take the hardening away and leave the imprint.

### Lingering Loans

If someone had borrowed an ability from the victim, with Borrow, or copied it with Thief's Ledger, it now matters how the victim died. A death that does not count **ends the loan or copy at once**. A death that counts leaves it: the time it had left is doubled (at most 2 minutes) and it works at a quarter more power. The holder gets one line saying the Nen outlived its owner. A copy taken from a mob is not tied to it and runs to its own timer.

## Dying with an oath

Two [oaths](Vows) change a death. They are for the player who swears them, and a killer meets their results.

**Second Beat.** A player sworn to it can survive one lethal blow an hour. Their health drops to 1 and they lie flat and still for 5 seconds. The death message is sent as if they had died, mobs lose them and nothing they do works. Only someone using Gyo sees a faint pulse rise from the body. A blow from a creature or player in that time kills them for real (fire and lava too); anything else leaves them lying. They wake with 2 hearts, no aura and a minute of Nen Exhaustion. A Second Beat is not a death, so none of this page happens for it: no body rises, no grudge follows the killer, and loans from that player are not touched.

**Last Will Oath.** A player sworn to it makes any living killer count, not only players and creatures with aura, and the risen body and orphaned beast hit up to twice as hard (the Death Grudge and the rest are as above). At Nen level 40 or higher their death also leaves a **Nen Will**: their signature [Hatsu](Hatsu), left where they fell for an heir to take up at the Meditation Stone, at 70% of its power until the heir reworks it. The oath is used up, and the player cannot swear it again for 7 real days. On a server where Nen is lost on death, a death of a player with a signature Hatsu leaves a Will, with or without the oath, but not again until 7 real days have passed since their last Will.

## For servers, M24c

`heartCooldownSeconds` (3600), `heartStillSeconds` (5), `heartHealth` (4), `heartExhaustionSeconds` (60), `lastWillGrowth` (2), `willReswearDays` (7), `nenWillScale` (0.7) and `nenWillLevel` (40) tune them.

## For servers, M24b

`grudgeTrailRadius` (64), `grudgeTrailSeconds` (60), `cursedWeaponChance` (0.5), `grudgeSealSeconds` (180), `hauntedSeconds` (1200), `maxHauntedSpots` (32), `hauntedAuraDrain` (2), `sealHardenScale` (2), `lingeringLoanMaxSeconds` (120) and `lingeringLoanPotency` (1.25) tune these, with the `postMortemAllySense`, `postMortemLingeringLoans`, `postMortemHardenGear`, `postMortemHardenSeals`, `postMortemCursedWeapon`, `postMortemSealedHatsu` and `postMortemHauntedGround` keys turning each on or off. All are listed in `docs/config-reference.md`.

## For servers

The settings are `postMortemEnabled` (true), `postMortemLevel` (25), `postMortemCooldownSeconds` (1200), `postMortemPairSeconds` (3600), `postMortemGates` (25, 30, 35, 40), `postMortemGrowth` (1.5), `remnantDelayTicks` (40), `remnantHit` (8), `maxRemnantScale` (2), `remnantSeconds` (15), `grudgeSeconds` (600), `grudgeRegenPenalty` (0.25), `orphanSeconds` (15) and `rogueRiseChance` (0.1), plus one `postMortem<Effect>` key per effect. `postMortemEnabled` off turns off the body, the grudge, the orphan beast and rising mobs together. Setting `rogueRiseChance` to 0 stops only rising Rogues and wild beasts. See [Server Owners](Server-Owners).
