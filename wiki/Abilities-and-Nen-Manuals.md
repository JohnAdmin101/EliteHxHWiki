# Abilities and Nen Manuals

Techniques are the basics. **Abilities** are the moves: a strike that hits harder, an orb of aura, a blade made of Nen. You learn them from **Nen Manuals**, bind them to slots and use them with a key.

## Getting a Nen Manual

Each manual teaches one ability and is used up when you learn from it. Hold it and right-click.

| Source | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Chests | Dungeon chests (25% of the time), abandoned mineshafts (15%), stronghold libraries (40%) and ancient cities (30%) can hold a manual. |
| Librarians | An expert librarian may sell one for 24 emeralds and a book. About two librarians in five offer it, and the ability is random. |
| Inscribing | Craft a Blank Nen Manual (a book and an amethyst shard) and right-click it on a Meditation Stone. It costs 50 aura and 5 experience levels, and gives you a random ability of your own type that you do not know yet. Specialists have nothing to inscribe. |

Creative mode has a **Nen** tab with the Meditation Stone, a Blank Nen Manual, one manual for every ability that a manual can teach and the Nen Reset Token.

A manual's name tells you the ability ("Nen Manual: Aura Bolt"), and its tooltip shows the type and the memory cost. A "Faded Nen Manual" teaches nothing, because its ability is gone (for example, from a removed addon). Manuals can never teach pool abilities, because those come from your type's pool, one per player.

If a manual does not work, the action bar says why: you have not awakened, you already know it, you have no affinity for its type, or you are out of memory (see below).

The three beast abilities are not pool abilities: a manual teaches them to any type, and they use 4 to 5 memory when they match your type. The rows from Thief's Ledger on are **pool** abilities, four for each type: awakening gives a player one from the pool of their type at random, and no manual teaches them. Thief's Ledger's copy takes the ledger's slot, uses no memory, gains no mastery and is given back when it expires (3 minutes), on logout, and on death when the server wipes Nen at death (by default Nen is kept, and so is the copy until it expires). Nen Severed blocks every technique, Zetsu included, and every ability. See [Nen Types and Affinity](Nen-Types-and-Affinity).

Addons can add more. An ability of another type costs the same aura but has less power (see [Nen Types and Affinity](Nen-Types-and-Affinity)).

## Slots

An ability does nothing until you bind it to a slot. Your slots unlock with your Nen level:

| Slot | Unlocks at Nen level |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 10 |
| 4 | 15 |

Press **K** to open the binding screen. Click an ability, then click a slot. Right-click a slot to clear it. Nothing binds itself when you learn it. A slot never locks again once you have it. Servers can add up to two more slots, so you may see slots 5 and 6.

The slot keys are unbound at first. Bind them in Options, Controls, in the **Nen** category ("Ability slot 1" and so on). Press the key to use the ability. For charge and channel abilities (Aura Bolt, Aura Tether) hold the key and release it. Each press counts once, even if you keep holding.

The slots show along the bottom of your screen as the ability bar: a sweep for the cooldown, a glow while it runs, and a red tint when you cannot afford it. It hides when you hide the HUD (F1).

### To use an ability you need

- To be awakened, and the ability learned and bound.
- Ten on. Abilities need it, so they will not work while Zetsu is on.
- Enough aura, and the cooldown finished.
- No Nen Exhaustion and no Vow Lockout.

If any of these fail, the action bar tells you which.

## Memory

You can only remember so much Nen. **Memory** is a limit on how many abilities you can know at once.

- You start with 10 memory. It grows with your level, up to 60 at the level cap.
- Each ability uses some of it. The built-in ones use 2 to 4 when they match your type (Aura Read, a Specialization pool ability, uses 2). The three beast abilities use 4 or 5. Abilities that are granted to you count toward the total too. A type further from yours costs more: the cost is divided by your affinity and rounded up. A tooltip on every manual shows the base cost.
- A manual is refused if learning it would put you over your limit. The message gives the cost and what you have free.
- Your signature Hatsu also uses memory (usually 12 to 20).

The binding screen shows "Memory used / total". It turns red if you are over.

### Forgetting

In the binding screen, select an ability and click **Forget** (it asks you to click twice, because you lose its mastery). Its memory stays used for one in-game day (about 20 minutes) before it frees up, so swapping has a real cost. Your type's pool ability, such as Aura Read, cannot be forgotten, and neither can an ability with a locked vow on it.

## Mastery

Every ability has a mastery. It rises by a little each time you finish using it, and it starts to matter as you practise:

- A new ability works at half strength. Mastery brings it up to full strength.
- The cap is your affinity for the ability's type. Your own type reaches 100%, and an opposite type stops at 40%.
- Types far from yours are slower to learn. An opposite-type ability takes 2.5 times the uses to reach its cap.
- Each completed use gives 5 Nen XP too (less if you keep using the same ability on the same target within a minute).

The hub and the binding screen show mastery as a percentage of your cap. Reaching it with an ability of your own type earns the **Mastery** advancement.
