# Hatsu

Manuals teach you the abilities everybody can learn. **Hatsu** is the one that is only yours: a signature ability you design yourself. You earn the right with a short trial at your Meditation Stone, then build the ability in a forge screen.

## The trial

You can take the trial at Nen level 15 (a server can change that). Crouch and right-click your Meditation Stone with both hands empty. A dialog offers the trial. It takes about two minutes and has two parts.

**1. Endurance.** Start Ren within 10 seconds, then hold it for 30 seconds without letting your aura drop below a quarter of your maximum. Start with a full pool. At level 15 your pool is 175 aura, and the hold uses about two thirds of it.

**2. Sight.** Between 3 and 7 marks, hidden with aura, appear in the air around the stone. They are shown only to you, and only while Gyo is on, for 20 seconds. Switch Gyo on from the technique wheel (the dialog and the message tell you which key opens it), count the marks, and then answer in the dialog within a minute.

If you fail, you wait 10 minutes before you can try again. These all count as failing:

- A wrong count, or no answer in time.
- Letting Ren end, or letting your aura fall under the floor.
- Walking more than 8 blocks from the stone.
- Breaking the stone yourself, dying, logging out or changing dimension.

If the stone has no room for the marks (they need open air that you can see from above the stone), the trial is cancelled and you can try again at once.

Passing earns the **Hatsu** advancement and opens the forge.

Specialists get one extra choice when they pass: pick a form to open at the stone, once.

## The forge

You use the same stone, with crouch and both hands empty, any time after you pass. The forge screen has these tabs:

- A **form**: the shape of the ability. Pick one.
- **Effects**: what it does. Pick two (a third opens at 50% mastery and a fourth at 100%, each in place of one more stage; see Stages).
- **Modifiers**: small changes to one number of your form, such as a longer reach or an extra shot. Click one to add a stack, click again for more, and past its limit it comes off. Each costs memory and some more aura per stack, and the forge greys out the ones your form cannot use and says why. See below.
- A **trigger**: when it fires. Pick one.
- **Vows**: up to eight, built into the ability. They add power without using memory.

Below the tabs is a live preview of the cost, the cooldown and the damage "vs mobs" and "vs players". Give your Hatsu a name (up to 32 characters) or press Randomize for a name that suits your type. Forge it, and it joins your abilities. Bind it to a slot like any other ability.

### Forms

| Form | Home type | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Projectile | Emission | A shot that travels and hits |
| Melee rider | Enhancement | Rides on your melee hits for a while |
| Construct | Conjuration | A conjured blade, Aura Pickaxe (mines like a diamond pickaxe, never wears out) or shield |
| Field | Transmutation | A zone around you while you channel |
| Tether | Manipulation | A link to one target |
| Self-empower | Enhancement | Buffs only you |
| Touch | Enhancement | A hit on one target within reach |
| Beam | Emission | A line that hits the first target it crosses, and more with Pierce |
| Wave | Emission | A cone or ring that spreads outward and hits each creature once |
| Trap | Conjuration | A hidden sigil that bursts when something steps near it |
| Orbit | Transmutation | Motes circling you that strike what comes close, for a few seconds |

A form is open to you when it matches your type, or when you have learned the preset ability that opens it: Aura Bolt opens Projectile, Hardened Strike opens Melee rider, Aura Construct opens Construct, Shock Edge opens Field and Aura Tether opens Tether. Self-empower is open to Enhancers only. The newer forms (Touch, Beam, Wave, Trap, Orbit) are not opened by type: unlock them in the Compendium (below). Not every trigger fits every form, and the forge greys out what does not and tells you why.

### Effects

Impact (raw damage), Shockwave (knockback), Element (fire, shock or frost), Bind (slow, then root), Pull or push, Command (a struck mob attacks another creature near it), Mark (reveal the target, even through walls, and make later hits on it stronger), Drain (move aura from the target to you), Restore (heal you and teammates close by), Barrier (absorption hearts), Echo (the effect repeats once, at half damage) and Condition-bound (stronger against one kind of target, weaker against everything else).

Newer effects: Lifesteal (heal a share of the damage dealt), Haste (Speed and Haste for you), Cleanse (remove your harmful vanilla effects, not Nen ones), Blink (teleport to where the Hatsu lands, only to a safe spot in view), Weaken, Gravity (lift or slam the target), Disarm (a mob loses its weapon, which drops only if the mob would always drop it; a player's held item swaps with another hotbar slot), Erode (ignores half of the target's Ren and Ken), Terrain (dig soft blocks or raise plain ground, never copying ore, where it lands), Grow (bonemeal crops, stems, berry bushes and cocoa where it lands), Silence (the target cannot use abilities for a few seconds; techniques still work), Conceal (hide from En and from mobs for a few seconds; Gyo still sees you) and Decoy (a false copy of you that draws hostile mobs). Blink, Terrain and Grow act where the first stage lands, so they need a form that can lead a chain, and Terrain and Grow never change blocks in a claim you may not build in, in a Nen zone, in spawn protection or where another protection mod says no, nor on bedrock and obsidian.

Specialists also get Foresight (see a target's cooldowns and techniques), Borrow (borrow an ability the target knows for 30 seconds) and Exchange (swap places or aura with the target). A borrowed ability depends on its owner: if the player you borrowed it from dies, the loan ends at once, unless their death was a strong Nen user's last stand, in which case it lingers with its time doubled (at most 2 minutes) and a quarter more power. Thief's Ledger copies follow the same rule. See [Post-mortem Nen](Post-mortem-Nen).

### Modifiers

| Modifier | What each stack does | Most stacks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | More power for the whole Hatsu | 3 |
| Range | Reaches 30% further | 3 |
| Radius | A wider area | 3 |
| Duration | Lasts 30% longer | 3 |
| Speed | A faster shot | 2 |
| Pierce | A shot passes through one more target | 2 |
| Split | One more shot, each hitting for 70% | 2 |
| Homing | A shot turns toward a target | 1 |
| Bounce | A shot bounces on once more, each bounce for 70% | 2 |
| Delay | Takes hold 2 seconds later, but hits harder | 2 |
| Efficient | Costs 15% less aura | 2 |
| Quick | Ready again 15% sooner | 2 |

Each modifier belongs to a Nen type. One of another type costs more memory and weighs on your Hatsu's affinity like any other off-type part. Not every form takes every modifier: the Projectile takes most of them, the Field, the Tether and the buffs only a few, and a Hatsu that fires on your melee hits takes only Power, Efficient and Quick.

### Stages

A Hatsu starts with one stage. At 50% mastery you earn one upgrade and at 100% mastery a second, and each upgrade buys either one more effect or one more **stage**. A stage is a form with its own effects and modifiers: the second stage starts where the first one lands (the target a shot hits or the spot it strikes), then a third can follow the second. Builds you can reach: 1 stage with 2 effects at the start; 1 stage with 3 effects, or 2 stages with 2 effects, at 50%; 1 with 4, 2 with 3, or 3 stages with 2 effects at 100%. A later stage may have no effects, but then it deals only its form's own damage. An effect can be on one stage only.

In the forge, a strip under the name box shows the stages. Click one to edit it, press + to add a stage and - to remove the selected one. The Form, Effects and Modifiers tabs then work on that stage; the trigger and vows belong to the whole Hatsu.

- A chain starts on the **first** landing of a stage: an entity it hits, or a block it strikes. Later hits of the same stage, such as a piercing or split shot, never start it again, so each stage fires once a use.
- Only the Projectile, Field, Touch, Beam, Wave and Trap can be a later stage (a Trap is placed at the landing). A Projectile stage fires from the landing point at the nearest other target in sight, or along your look. A Field stage is a zone at the landing point that pulses for a few seconds and costs no upkeep. Self-empower can lead a chain and starts the next stage at once from you. Construct and the Nen beast form cannot lead one, and the Melee rider and Tether cannot follow one.
- Each later stage costs 35% more aura, and hits at three quarters of the power of the stage before it.
- One use affects at most 12 different targets. Against players, one player can deal another at most 14 health of Hatsu damage in two seconds, across all their Hatsu; effects still apply.
- A stage that would start while you are in Zetsu or a Nen zone is lost.

### Triggers

Instant (fires on press), Charge (hold to scale the power; a full charge hits a quarter harder than Instant), On-hit (fires on your next melee hits, up to 3 per use), Conditional (fires only when a condition holds: low health, a marked target or night, and cheaper because of it) and Delayed (fires on press, but takes hold 3 seconds later; only for a form that takes Delay).

**Armed triggers** do nothing at the press except arm your Hatsu for a while; it then fires by itself when its signal comes, with a pause of half a second between fires. Pulse fires every 4 seconds (3 times in 20 seconds), On hurt when you lose at least 1 health (3 times in 20 seconds), On kill when you kill a creature (3 times in 30 seconds), Last stand once when your health falls to 30% or less (within 60 seconds) and On landing when you land from a fall of 3 blocks or more (3 times in 30 seconds). Arming costs aura and upkeep, but each fire is free. Projectile, Beam, Wave, Field, Touch and Self-empower can be armed; the Melee rider, Tether, Construct, Beast, Trap and Orbit cannot. A Hatsu never sets off its own armed Hatsu (a server can allow that with `hatsuReactiveDepth`), and nothing fires while you are in Zetsu or a Nen zone, or under Vow Lockout, Nen Exhaustion, Nen Severed or Silence.

### Memory and overcharge

Each part costs memory, cheaper for parts of your own type. Your Hatsu must fit in your free memory. Leftover memory can go into overcharge, which adds 5% power per point, up to 10 points.

## The Compendium

Parts that arrived after the first builder (the Touch, Beam, Wave, Trap and Orbit forms, the newer effects and triggers, and every modifier) are closed until you unlock them with **Insight**. Open the **Compendium** from the Hatsu tab of the status screen, or with its button in the builder (between Forge and Cancel). It shows a column per branch: Enhancement, Emission, Transmutation, Conjuration, Manipulation, Triggers, Specialization (never buyable: only Specialists can use those) and Other (parts from addons). Lines join a part to the parts it needs first.

- **Earning Insight.** One point for every 2 Nen levels, and 2 more the first time each Hatsu's mastery reaches half and again when it is full (once for each slot, ever). A server can change the rates.
- **Spending it.** Click a part you can afford, then click it again to confirm. It costs 1 Insight in your own branch, 2 in a neighbouring one and 3 farther away on the type ring (1 if you have no type, and 1 for triggers). A part needs the parts joined to it in its branch first. A part already open to you does not show.
- **Refunds.** At a Meditation Stone, the Refund button gives back every part you bought that none of your Hatsu uses, then you wait 3 in-game days (a server can change this) for the next one. A part a Hatsu uses is never refunded.
- **Your old Hatsu.** A Hatsu you forged before the Compendium keeps working and keeps its parts, free, and a rework may keep them too. Taking up a Nen Will never needs the unlock either.

## Several Hatsu

You can forge up to three Hatsu, one for each slot. The slots open at Nen level 15, 30 and 45 (a server can change this, or allow fewer slots). The trial is taken once and covers every slot. Open the Meditation Stone and the builder shows three tabs: a locked tab says when it opens, an empty one starts a new forge, and each slot keeps its own build, name, mastery and vows. You can forge them in any order.

The Hatsu run together: each has its own cost, cooldown and upkeep, and each is bound to the ability bar on its own. The same part does not stack with itself across Hatsu (two Self-empowers give the stronger one, not both), but different parts add up. All of your Hatsu share one memory pool, so a second and third build need room left over. If a server later closes a slot, that Hatsu is suspended until it opens again; it is never deleted. Reworking one slot does not touch the others.

## Changing it later

- **Rename** it for free from the binding screen (the Forget button becomes Rename when your Hatsu is selected).
- **Rework** it at the stone. A rework keeps only half of your mastery, and you cannot rework again for 3 in-game days (about an hour).

## The Nen Will

When a Nen user with a signature Hatsu dies and Nen is lost on death, they leave a **Nen Will** where they fell. A server that keeps Nen on death leaves one only when the dead player was sworn to the Last Will Oath, was counted as a post-mortem death and was at Nen level 40 or higher. See [Vows](Vows) and [Post-mortem Nen](Post-mortem-Nen).

Pick the Will up, hold it in your main hand and talk to the Meditation Stone: "Take up the Nen Will in your hand". A Will holds every Hatsu its maker had forged. If it holds more than one, a dialog asks which one you take up; you take one build per Will, and it goes into your lowest free slot. The Hatsu is judged like a new forge, except that the effect and stage limit of a new Hatsu does not apply, so a whole three-stage build can pass on. You must still be awakened, have passed the trial, have a free open slot, have the memory for it, and have every part available to you. A refusal tells you why and keeps the Will. The name is checked like any other name.

You get the maker's Hatsu at **70% of its power until you rework it**. A rework builds your own and drops the reduction, but it must fit your own mastery: the builder warns you when parts would be removed. The Will is used up when you take it. You earn the **Heir** advancement.

## Fighting other players

On many servers, Hatsu do less damage to players than to mobs. The default is 60% against players, and the forge preview shows the number for the server you are on. A server can set it to 0, and then your Hatsu leaves other players alone entirely. Whatever the number, your Hatsu never hurts a player you could not hurt with a plain hit (PvP off, or a teammate with friendly fire off).

Killing a strong Nen user can seal one of your abilities for a while: the ability that struck the fatal blow, or your best-mastered one. See [Post-mortem Nen](Post-mortem-Nen).

Forging your first Hatsu earns the **Signature** advancement. Read [Vows](Vows) if you want to build one in.
