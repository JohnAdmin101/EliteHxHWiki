# Cursed Items

Some gear in the world is better than it looks, and worse. A cursed item is an ordinary sword, axe or armor piece with leftover Nen clinging to it. It gives you a strong boon while you use it, and a bane you cannot ignore. The curse is hidden until you first use the item, unless you look with Gyo.

## Finding them

Cursed gear is rare chest loot: iron or diamond swords, axes and armor, already enchanted, in simple dungeons, abandoned mineshafts, stronghold corridors, ancient cities and bastion treasure rooms. Bastions and ancient cities are the best bets. You will not meet it anywhere else.

## Seeing a curse

- **First use.** The first time a cursed item sits in a slot it fits (a weapon in your main hand, armor on its slot) the curse shows itself. Chat says "A leftover aura clings to you" with its name, and from then on the item's tooltip lists the curse, its boon and its bane in purple. Creative players get the boon but never the bane.
- **With Gyo.** Turn Gyo on and the tooltip of an item in your own inventory shows the curse before you ever use it, marked "Seen with Gyo". Cursed items lying on the ground in sight and within Gyo's range give off a few dark particles, and only players with Gyo on see them. Gyo does not read what other players wear or hold.

A curse also comes from a fight. When you kill a strong Nen user in melee, the weapon in your hand may take one of these curses, hidden until it shows itself. See [Post-mortem Nen](Post-mortem-Nen).

## The three curses

| Curse | Fits | Boon | Bane |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bloodthirst | Swords and axes | +3 attack damage | Loses 1 health every 6 seconds, but never takes you below 2 hearts. |
| Grudge | Any armor piece | +3 armor and +2 armor toughness | Cannot be taken off. |
| Beacon | Swords, axes and armor | +10% movement speed on armor, +15% attack speed on a weapon | Hostile mobs within 16 blocks that can see you turn on you, checked every 2 seconds. |

Bloodthirst's drain is damage, so it also ends a Meditation Stone awakening meditation: put the sword away to meditate.

Boons and banes last only while you hold or wear the item. Put the weapon away and both end, apart from Grudge, which will not let go of the armor. Zetsu hides you from the Beacon: monsters ignore you while it is on. The server owner can change every number above (see [Server Owners](Server-Owners)).

## Getting rid of a curse

There are two ways, and neither can be used on you without your say-so.

- **The Meditation Stone.** Hold a cursed item whose curse has shown itself and use the stone: the menu offers "Cleanse the held curse". It costs 10 experience levels (none in creative). If you do not have enough, the button still shows and nothing is taken. The item keeps its other enchantments. A Grudge piece you are wearing cannot be held, so the stone cannot lift it; you need an exorcist.
- **Exorcism.** Specialists can roll the ability [Exorcism](Abilities-and-Nen-Manuals). It lifts every curse from the worn and held items of another player in sight within 6 blocks, including a worn Grudge piece. It costs 40 aura and has a 2 minute cooldown.

Exorcism never works on yourself and never on someone who has not accepted. The target must be on your team, or crouching while looking at you, when you use it. Crouching while looking somewhere else does not count, so sneaking past a Specialist never gives your curses away. If they are standing and are not a teammate, the ability tells you they must accept, and costs you nothing. Because it asks the target's consent, nobody can strip another player's boon against their will.

## Ending broken-vow penalties

Both removal paths also end the timers of a broken vow. At the stone, "Settle a broken vow" costs 15 experience levels and shows only while Vow Lockout is running or a broken vow is still being paid for. Exorcism does the same for its target. Aura you already lost stays lost; only the waiting ends. See [Vows](Vows).

The stone's Settle and Exorcism also end the **seal on a killer's Hatsu** that a fallen Nen user can leave behind, and Exorcism also lifts a **hardening** from an infused piece (and a haunted spot: with no player in sight, it clears the spot close to you). See [Post-mortem Nen](Post-mortem-Nen).

Gear can also carry your own Nen on purpose, without a curse. A cursed piece will not take the ritual until you lift the curse. See [Infused Gear](Infused-Gear).
