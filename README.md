# Elite Hunter X Hunter

Elite HxH adds Nen to Minecraft. You wake your aura at a Meditation Stone, learn to wrap it around your body, to hide it, to spread it out and to flare it, and then you learn abilities of your own. Your aura is visible: steam in your Nen type's color pours off your body, and it changes with what you are doing. The screens and the HUD are built to match, so everything you do with Nen looks the part.

The mod is for NeoForge, Minecraft 26.3. It needs GeckoLib, and every client also needs Player Animation Library. It works in singleplayer and on servers, and other mods can add abilities, vows and effects through a public addon API.

## New here?

Start with [Getting Started](wiki/Getting-Started). It walks you through your first hour: crafting the Meditation Stone, waking your Nen, your first technique, your first ability and your first vow. Everything is also guided in game by the **Nen** tab of your advancements screen (press L): each entry tells you how to earn it, so the tab points you to the next milestone.

## What you can do

- Wake your Nen and get a Nen type. See [Awakening](wiki/Awakening).
- Grow your Nen level, up to Nen level 50 by default. Each level adds aura and some unlock new techniques and ability slots.
- Use the six core techniques: Ten, Zetsu, Ren, Gyo, Ken and En. See [Techniques and the Wheel](wiki/Techniques-and-the-Wheel).
- Learn abilities from Nen Manuals and use them in a fight. See [Abilities and Nen Manuals](wiki/Abilities-and-Nen-Manuals).
- Practise Ten, Ren and Gyo with drills at the stone. See [Training Drills](wiki/Training-Drills).
- Pass the Hunter Exam for a Hunter License. See [Hunter Exam](wiki/Hunter-Exam).
- Swear vows for extra power, and pay for breaking them. See [Vows](wiki/Vows).
- Summon a creature of your own aura to fight beside you. See [Nen Beasts](wiki/Nen-Beasts).
- Find cursed gear in chests, and lift its curse. See [Cursed Items](wiki/Cursed-Items).
- Pour your Nen into a sword, tool or armor piece for good. See [Infused Gear](wiki/Infused-Gear).
- Learn what a fallen Nen user leaves behind. See [Post-mortem Nen](wiki/Post-mortem-Nen).
- Pass the Hatsu trial and forge a signature Hatsu of your own. See [Hatsu](wiki/Hatsu).

## A note on names

Nen, Ten, Zetsu, Ren, Gyo, En, Ken, Hatsu and the six types are the ideas this mod is built on. Everything here, the text and the art, is original to the mod.
