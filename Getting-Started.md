# Getting Started

This page takes you through your first hour with Nen, in the order that works best. You do not need to memorize anything: the **Nen** tab in your advancements screen (press L) lists the main milestones on the way, such as awakening, your first techniques, your first ability and your first vow, and says how to earn each one. It does not cover the screens and keys, so come back to this page for those.

Nen is slow to grow on purpose. You can reach Nen level 50 at most (that is the default, and server owners can change it), and the early levels come from practice, not from a single big action.

## 1. Craft the Meditation Stone

The stone is how you wake your Nen. Craft it with five stone bricks and one amethyst shard, in two rows:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stone bricks | Amethyst shard | Stone bricks |
| Stone bricks | Stone bricks | Stone bricks |

Place it somewhere calm. You will come back to it again and again: you also use it to inscribe manuals and, much later, to take the Hatsu trial.

## 2. Meditate

Right-click the stone (without crouching) and stay still. (Once you are awake, the same click opens a menu with **Meditate** and your training drills; choose Meditate to meditate.) The action bar counts the seconds. It takes 30 seconds, and you have to stay within one block of where you started. Moving away, crouching, taking damage or breaking the stone cancels it, and you can just start again.

When the meditation ends, a glass of water appears over the stone. Its animation shows your Nen type, and a chat line names it. You are awake: you are at Nen level 0, your aura is full, and your first technique, Ten, is already on. You will see the status panel in the top left of your screen, with your aura bar, and steam in your type's color starts to rise from your body. See [Awakening](Awakening) for the other ways to wake and for what the type means.

## 3. Open the status hub (N)

Press **N**. The hub shows your type, level, XP and aura on the Status tab, with your own model beside it wearing your aura. The other tabs list your techniques, abilities, vows and Hatsu. The hub only shows things, so it is safe to click around. Come back to it whenever you want to know what you can do next: locked techniques show the level that unlocks them.

## 4. Open the technique wheel (R)

Press **R**. A ring of eight slots opens: Ten, Ken, Ryu, Shu, Gyo, In, En and Zetsu. Click one to switch it on or off. Locked ones show the level they need. At the start only Ten is yours; Zetsu arrives at level 2 and Gyo at level 8. See [Techniques and the Wheel](Techniques-and-the-Wheel) for what each one does and when it unlocks.

The wheel is the main way to use techniques. The keys for Ten, Zetsu, Gyo, Ken and En are unbound until you choose to bind them.

## 5. Grow, and flare your aura with Ren (C)

Your level grows with Nen XP. Early on, the easy sources are finishing another meditation at the stone (15 XP each), passing a [training drill](Training-Drills) and using abilities (5 XP per completed use). XP is capped at 60 a minute, so patience is part of the design. Level 2 needs 250 XP in total and level 4 needs 700.

At level 4 you unlock **Ren**. Hold **C** to flare your aura: the steam swells, you take less damage, and your abilities hit harder the longer you hold. Ren costs aura while you hold it, and it is also a slow way to earn XP (1 per second while it runs). Release C to stop.

When you reach a level that unlocks something, a pop-up names it and says which key or screen to use. Slots and vow warnings get a pop-up too. You can turn them off with `unlockToasts` in `client.toml`.

## 6. Find a Nen Manual

Abilities come from Nen Manuals, one ability per manual. You have three ways to get one:

- **Loot.** Manuals turn up in dungeon, abandoned mineshaft, stronghold library and ancient city chests.
- **A librarian.** An expert librarian may offer one for 24 emeralds and a book.
- **Inscribing.** Craft a Blank Nen Manual (a book and an amethyst shard), then right-click it on your Meditation Stone. It becomes a manual for a random ability of your own type. It costs 50 aura and 5 experience levels.

Right-click a manual to learn its ability. The manual is used up. Details are on [Abilities and Nen Manuals](Abilities-and-Nen-Manuals).

## 7. Bind the ability (K)

A new ability is not on your ability bar yet. Press **K** to open the binding screen, click the ability, then click a slot. You start with one slot, and more unlock at levels 4, 10 and 15. Right-click a slot to clear it.

To use a slot in play, bind a key to it in the controls screen (Options, Controls, the **Nen** category, "Ability slot 1" and so on). Press that key to use the ability. Abilities need Ten to be on, and they cost aura, so keep an eye on the bar.

## 8. Swear a first vow

A vow is a promise that makes an ability stronger and costs you if you break it. Open the vows screen from the hub's Vows button (or from the binding screen), pick an oath, pick the ability to swear it on and confirm. A gentle first choice is the Offering Oath, which asks for one amethyst shard each time you use the ability. Breaking a vow for the first time is only a warning. See [Vows](Vows).

## After the first hour

- Reach Nen level 10 for Ken and a third ability slot, and level 12 for En.
- Reach Nen level 25 to earn the **Adept** advancement.
- Reach Nen level 15 for the Hatsu trial and the fourth slot. Passing the trial lets you forge a signature Hatsu of your own. See [Hatsu](Hatsu).
- Reach the top Nen level (level 50 by default) to earn the last milestone in the **Nen** tab, Limit Reached.

If something does not work, [FAQ and Troubleshooting](FAQ-and-Troubleshooting) has the common answers. All the keys are on [Controls](Controls).
