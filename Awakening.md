# Awakening

Until you awaken, you cannot use any Nen. You have no aura bar, the technique wheel stays closed (pressing **R** only tells you to meditate), and the status hub (**N**) shows one tab with a hint on how to wake. Awakening happens once per character.

## What you start with

When you awaken you get:

- Nen level 0, with full aura (100 at the start; every level adds 5).
- A Nen type, rolled at random. All six types are equally likely by default. A glass of water appears and plays an animation for your type, then a chat line names it. See [Nen Types and Affinity](Nen-Types-and-Affinity).
- **Ten**, learned and switched on.
- One ability slot.

You do not start with any abilities, unless your type has a pool (every type does): you get one ability from your type's pool, picked at random (see [Nen Types and Affinity](Nen-Types-and-Affinity)). Everything else comes from [Nen Manuals](Abilities-and-Nen-Manuals).

## Route 1: meditate at a Meditation Stone

This is the normal way.

1. Craft a Meditation Stone: five stone bricks and one amethyst shard (see [Getting Started](Getting-Started)).
2. Right-click it without crouching.
3. Stay still for 30 seconds, within one block of where you started. The action bar shows your progress.

Moving away, crouching, taking damage or breaking the stone cancels the meditation, and you can start again. When the 30 seconds end, you awaken.

If you are already awake, using the stone opens a menu instead. Choose **Meditate** to run the same 30-second meditation, which gives you 15 XP of practice, or choose a training drill (see [Training Drills](Training-Drills)).

## Route 2: forced awakening

Another player can wake you by force, if the server allows it. It is meant for story servers and is deliberately costly, because it hurts.

- The player who forces it must be at least Nen level 10, with Ren active. They crouch and right-click you.
- You see a popup that asks whether you want to awaken. It stays open for 30 seconds, and Escape declines. Nothing happens unless you accept.
- If you accept, you awaken at once with no aura, you lose half of your current health (it never kills you) and you are hit by Nen Exhaustion, which slows you for a short time. Ten is learned but starts switched off.
- The player who forced it spends half of their maximum aura. If you decline, they spend nothing.
- After a decline or a popup that runs out, nobody can ask you again for 60 seconds.

Forcing someone awake earns the **Mentor** advancement. Server owners can turn this route off, see [Server Owners](Server-Owners).

## Route 3: an admin command

Operators can use `/nen admin awaken <player> [type]`, which can also pick the type. It is for testing and for server events. See [Server Owners](Server-Owners).

## After you die

By default you keep your Nen when you die. If a server turns that off, you lose your awakening but remember your type, and meditating again wakes you with the same type unless the server allows re-rolls. The signature Hatsu you forged is kept either way.

## Changing your type

You normally keep the type you rolled. A rare **Nen Reset Token**, found in ancient city and end city treasure chests, can re-roll your type, but only on servers that allow it, and it is switched off by default.
